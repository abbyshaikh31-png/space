Gemini is one universal agent for the way you work. When using Gemini at work, it operates across your email, docs, code, data, and business apps across web, desktop, and mobile, so you can hand off the work instead of supervising it. There is no platform to stand up and no agent to assemble: you turn it on and it starts working with what you already have from the same prompt box, with the same shared context. Do I have to move our data into Google? No. Gemini indexes selected sources while keeping data where it lives, and access strictly follows each user's existing permissions. Over 100 supported integrations like Google Workspace (Docs, Gmail, and Drive), Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, and OneDrive), Slack, Confluence, Notion, Jira, Linear, Monday, Asana, Smartsheet, GitHub, GitLab, Salesforce, HubSpot, ServiceNow, Docusign, and BigQuery. Who owns our data, and does Google use my data to train models? When using Gemini for your business, Gemini is built on Google Cloud’s secure-by-design infrastructure, giving you the peace of mind to deploy AI across your organization confidently: You own your data, not Google Your data, including prompts, outputs, and model tuning, is never used to train Google models or models for any other customer Google never sells customer data to third parties Google doesn’t use customer data for advertising What does Gemini remember about me? Session memory for active tasks, semantic memory for structured business knowledge, procedural memory for workflows, and episodic memory of past actions across devices and channels. Lengthy jobs also run on their own secure machine in Google Cloud, so even if you log off for the day, Gemini has the work done when you get back. Which models can I use? Google Gemini models today, with additional models coming soon. Gemini uses automated routing to select the optimal model for speed, cost, and accuracy, all under one set of permissions and one bill.
