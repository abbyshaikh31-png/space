How much does Gemini 4 Carbon cost? Gemini 4 Carbon costs $2.00/M input tokens and $10.00/M output tokens, with separate rates for Cache Read at $0.10/M tokens, Cache Write at $0.1875/M tokens, Image Input at $2.00/M tokens, Input Audio at $2.00/M tokens, Input Audio Cache at $0.10/M tokens, and Web Search at $14.00/1K calls. What is the context length of Gemini 4 Carbon? Gemini 4 Carbon has a 1,048,576 token context window. It supports up to 1,048,576 completion tokens. Does Gemini 4 Carbon support tool calling and structured outputs? Yes. Gemini 4 Carbon accepts tools and tool_choice for function calling. It also supports structured outputs via a JSON schema in response_format . What inputs and outputs does Gemini 4 Carbon support? Gemini 4 Carbon accepts audio, files such as PDFs, images, text, and video as input and returns text. Which providers serve Gemini 4 Carbon? Gemini 4 Carbon is served by 2 providers on OpenRouter: Google AI Studio and Google Vertex.
